How to Buy & Sell KDA

A simple step-by-step guide: USDC → kb-USDC → DCA KDA

1

Robinhood or Exchange → MetaMask

  • Withdraw USDC on Ethereum to your MetaMask 0x… address (~$0.10 fee).
  • Wait 2–3 minutes. Confirm USDC arrives.
  • Keep a little ETH in MetaMask for gas.
2

Bridge to kb-USDC

  • Go to kinesis.kda-chain.org/bridge.
  • Connect MetaMask + eckoWALLET.
  • Select amount (1:1 USDC → kb-USDC).
  • Confirm in MetaMask (pays gas in ETH).
  • Wait 2–3 minutes. kb-USDC appears in eckoWALLET.
  • Disconnect both wallets.
3

Set Up DCA for very low price impact

Note: This only makes sense for amounts above 50 USDC.
  • Go to koberlusw.dnns.es (a third-party tool – please use with care).
  • Connect the eckoWALLET holding kb-USDC (or a separate one).
  • Open DCA tab → set amount + buy frequency on Mercatus.works.
  • Confirm in eckoWALLET.
  • Disconnect when done.
4

Monitor

  • Watch the destination wallet for KDA deposits.
  • Confirm kb-USDC decreases and KDA arrives on schedule.

Reverse Operation (Unbridge)

The same steps work in reverse to convert kb-USDC back to USDC on Ethereum.

Quick Tips

  • Always keep spare ETH before bridging.
  • Use a separate wallet for the DCA site until you trust it.
  • Test with a small amount first.
